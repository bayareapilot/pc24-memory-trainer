<b>ACE-39 — Transponder — Never Select STBY on the Ground</b><br><br>Why must the transponder not be set to STBY on the ground, and what should be selected instead?	The ACE transponder supplies AUTOMATIC air-to-ground (GND) switching, so the old practice of selecting STBY on the ground does NOT apply.
